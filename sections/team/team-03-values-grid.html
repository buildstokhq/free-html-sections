What we believe

Six values we actually use

Not posters on a wall. These are how we hire, decide and give feedback.

01

Care about the details

We sweat the small things, because that's what people notice.

02

Ship, then improve

Done and live beats perfect and planned. We iterate in public.

03

Say it plainly

Clear words over clever ones, in our product and with each other.

04

Win as a team

No heroes, no blame. We share the credit and the hard parts.

05

Leave it better

In code, in docs and in the world. Small improvements compound.

06

Have some fun

We take the work seriously, not ourselves.
